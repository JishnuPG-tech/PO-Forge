Design a premium, modern banking exam preparation web application called POForge.

Positioning:

POForge
Personal AI Banking Coach

The application is for serious banking exam aspirants preparing for:

* IBPS RRB PO
* IBPS PO
* SBI PO
* SBI Clerk
* RBI Assistant
* other banking exams

The product should feel like a professional exam platform, not a generic AI dashboard.

Take inspiration from the information density, clarity, exam usability, analytics depth, and professional structure commonly expected from serious banking preparation platforms, but do not copy another platform's branding, layouts, graphics, wording, or proprietary design.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Aim for:

* premium
* focused
* modern
* academic
* professional
* technically polished
* calm
* high information density
* strong hierarchy

Do not make it childish.

Do not use excessive gamification.

Do not overuse gradients.

Do not fill every surface with glassmorphism.

Do not make the AI experience visually dominate the product.

Use subtle motion only where it improves understanding.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Mobile-first.

The app must work beautifully on:

* Android phone
* tablet
* laptop
* desktop

Question mode must prioritize mobile usability.

Analytics can expand into richer desktop layouts.

ACCESSIBILITY

Support:

* readable typography
* keyboard navigation
* focus states
* sufficient contrast
* accessible controls
* responsive touch targets

Create reusable components and a consistent design system.

Build the interface as a serious production product, not a landing-page mockup.
